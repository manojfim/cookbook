3-Ingredient Cottage Cheese Bagels

Easy high-protein cottage cheese bagels, pitched as a quick busy-morning breakfast.

20 minutes at 375F plus 5-7 minutes at 400F

vegetarian contains dairy breakfast

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of salt
  • Egg wash, for brushing
  • Everything bagel seasoning (for one version)
  • Shredded cheddar cheese and sliced jalapenos (for the other version)

Method

  1. Mix the flour, cottage cheese, baking powder and a pinch of salt in a bowl and blend until smooth.
  2. Shape the dough into rings and place on a parchment-lined tray.
  3. Brush with egg wash.
  4. Make two versions: one topped with everything bagel seasoning, the other with shredded cheddar cheese and sliced jalapenos.
  5. Bake at 375F for 20 minutes, then at 400F for 5-7 minutes until golden and fluffy.
  6. Slice and serve.

Toddler note: Chewy bagel texture; cut into small pieces for a toddler and skip the jalapeno version for young children.

Amritsari Paneer Bhurji

Creamy Amritsari-style paneer bhurji built on a roasted besan and yogurt base; whole dish about 656 kcal and 51g protein.

about 15 minutes · Serves not stated (whole dish)

vegetarian contains dairy dinner lunch breakfast

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tbsp besan flour
  • 230 g Greek yogurt (1 cup)
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp amchur powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp coriander powder (as listed)
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tbsp cumin seeds
  • 10 g thinly sliced ginger
  • 1 chopped onion (80 g)
  • 2 slit green chillies
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • 200 g low-fat paneer, mashed
  • 100 ml skimmed milk
  • Coriander, for garnish
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi

Method

  1. Dry-roast the besan flour in a pan on low heat for 5 minutes until nutty; set aside.
  2. In a bowl mix the Greek yogurt with chilli powder, amchur powder, coriander powder and the roasted besan.
  3. Heat the ghee on medium heat, add the cumin seeds and sliced ginger.
  4. Add the chopped onion, green chillies and salt; saute 5 minutes.

  6. Add the yogurt mixture and cook 5 minutes.
  7. Add the mashed paneer and skimmed milk and mix.
  8. Garnish with coriander and kasuri methi and serve.

Banana Cottage Cheese Muffins

Soft banana and cottage cheese muffins made in a blender, pitched for toddlers.

22-25 minutes at 350F (175C) · Serves 6 muffins

vegetarian contains dairy breakfast snack

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (225 g) cottage cheese
  • 1 large ripe banana or 2 small bananas
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp (15 ml) avocado oil or melted butter
  • 3/4 cup (95 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp (4 g) baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 350F (175C) and lightly grease or line a 6-cup muffin pan.
  2. Add the eggs, cottage cheese, banana and avocado oil or melted butter to a blender; blend until smooth.
  3. Transfer to a bowl, add the flour and baking powder and gently fold just until combined; do not overmix.
  4. Divide evenly among the muffin cups, filling each about 3/4 full.
  5. Bake 22-25 minutes, until the tops are lightly golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool in the pan 5 minutes, then transfer.
  7. Note from the creator: if the batter seems too runny, add 1-2 tbsp (8-16 g) more flour. Store airtight in the refrigerator up to 3 days or freeze up to 2 months.

Toddler note: Soft muffin texture, good for toddler self-feeding; standard muffin size can be halved for a toddler.

  • 3 minced garlic cloves (for gravy)
  • Chopped onion (for gravy)
  • 1/2 tsp ginger garlic paste (for gravy)
  • 2 chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tsp chilli powder (for gravy)
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder (for gravy)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin powder (for gravy)
  • 1 tsp garam masala (for gravy)
  • Salt (for gravy)
  • 3 tbsp curd (for blending)
  • 5 g butter (to finish gravy)
  • Cilantro, to garnish
  • 5 g butter (for rice)
  • 3 minced garlic cloves (for rice)
  • 1 tsp chilli powder (for rice)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder (for rice)
  • Oregano (for rice)
  • Salt (for rice)

  • 300 ml water

  7. Toast the washed raw basmati rice.
  8. Add 300 ml water (1.5 times the rice), close the lid and cook.
  9. Serve the butter chicken with the flavoured rice.

Cauliflower Paneer Gravy

Creamy cauliflower paneer gravy with a tomato-Kashmiri chilli puree and whole spices; 480 kcal and 20g protein.

Prep 10 minutes (chilli soak) · about 20 minutes

vegetarian contains dairy dinner lunch

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 200 g cauliflower
  • 100 g paneer
  • 2 big tomatoes
  • 1 big onion, chopped
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • 4 cardamom
  • 3 cloves
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp jeera
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 3 Kashmiri chillies
  • Coriander leaf
  • Water
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cut the cauliflower into small florets, wash well and steam 5 minutes; set aside.
  2. Soak the Kashmiri chillies in hot water 10 minutes, then blend with the tomato into a puree; set aside.
  3. Heat the oil in a kadai.
  4. Add the jeera, cardamom, cinnamon sticks, bay leaf, cloves and onion; saute 3-4 minutes on high flame.
  5. Add the ginger garlic paste and saute 2 minutes on medium-high flame.
  6. When the colour starts to change, add the tomato puree, turmeric, Kashmiri red chilli powder, coriander powder and salt; mix 5-6 minutes on high flame.
  7. Blend the paneer with water into a paste, keep the flame low and add to the kadai; mix 3 minutes on medium-high flame.
  8. Add the steamed cauliflower and mix 2 minutes on high flame.
  9. Check the salt and switch off the flame.
  10. Add the garam masala, kasuri methi and coriander leaf, mix gently and serve.

Chicken, Mushroom and Broccoli Stir Fry

Under-20-minute sticky-spicy soy-honey-chilli-crisp stir fry with velveted chicken; 2 servings, about 240 kcal and 28g protein each.

Prep 10-15 minutes (marinade) · under 20 minutes · Serves 2 (about 220 g each)

non-vegetarian dinner quick

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 large chicken breast (about 250 g), sliced into strips
  • 1 tbsp arrowroot or corn flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp neutral oil (light olive oil)
  • 1 tsp oil (for stir fry)
  • 1.5 tbsp crushed ginger and garlic (combined)
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion or spring onion whites
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • Salt to taste
  • 1.5 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli crisp
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 2 tbsp spring onion greens, to finish

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken strips with arrowroot, baking soda, soy sauce, salt and pepper; rest 10-15 minutes.
  2. Pan-fry on high heat in a little oil until golden brown, max 2 minutes on each side; set aside.
  3. In the same pan add a little oil and saute the crushed ginger and garlic, then the onion or spring onion whites.
  4. Add the mushrooms, broccoli and salt; stir-fry on high flame until the broccoli is crisp-tender.
  5. Add the soy sauce and toss.
  6. Return the chicken to the pan with the chilli crisp and honey; toss until glossy, 1-2 more minutes.
  7. Finish with spring onion greens and serve hot.

Cottage Cheese Bhurji (Nidhi's version)

Quick homestyle cottage cheese curry with mustard, cumin, garlic, onion and tomato masala, about 10 minutes.

about 10 minutes

vegetarian contains dairy dinner lunch quick

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 box cottage cheese (48 oz)
  • 1-2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • A pinch of hing (asafoetida)
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp crushed garlic
  • 1 small tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish

Method

  1. Heat oil in a pan on medium heat.
  2. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add cumin seeds and a pinch of hing.
  4. Add the green chilli, garlic and onion; saute until the onions turn soft and lightly golden.
  5. Add the tomato and cook until soft and pulpy.
  6. Add the red chilli powder, turmeric, cumin coriander powder, garam masala, kasuri methi and salt; mix well and cook 2-3 minutes until the masala is rich.
  7. Add the cottage cheese and mix gently so it does not break too much.
  8. Cook 1-2 more minutes so the cottage cheese absorbs the flavors.
  9. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot with roti or rice.

Veggie Cheese Loaf

Baked veggie and cheddar loaf that hides egg and vegetables, pitched by the creator as a toddler breakfast and on-the-go snack (filed under adult because it came from the Diet Food collection).

about 40 minutes (50 minutes total)

vegetarian contains dairy breakfast snack

View recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 125 g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 100 ml milk
  • 110 g grated cheddar
  • 1 medium carrot
  • 1 medium courgette (zucchini)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180C.
  2. Grate the courgette and carrot and squeeze out the liquid.
  3. In a bowl whisk the eggs, flour, baking powder and milk until smooth.
  4. Add half the grated cheese to the batter and mix well.
  5. Add the grated vegetables and mix well.
  6. Pour the batter into a tin lined with baking paper.
  7. Top with the remaining cheese, pressing lightly into the batter.
  8. Bake about 40 minutes until golden brown outside and cooked through; if the top browns before the middle is done, cover with foil.

Toddler note: Creator pitches this for toddlers and little hands as an on-the-go snack; soft baked texture, cut into fingers.
